================================================================================
QUESTIONS TO ASK INTERVIEWERS AND HIRING MANAGERS
================================================================================
Purpose: The questions you ask at the end of each interview round are just as
         important as your answers. Good questions demonstrate strategic thinking,
         signal genuine interest, and help YOU make a better decision.
         This is a comprehensive bank of questions organized by round type.
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY YOUR QUESTIONS MATTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Interviewers ask themselves two questions about every candidate:
  1. "Can this person do the job?"
  2. "Do I want to work with this person?"

Your closing questions influence both. A sharp, specific question signals:
  - You've researched the company
  - You think strategically about engineering
  - You're evaluating them as much as they're evaluating you (confidence)
  - You're genuinely interested, not just going through motions

QUESTIONS YOU SHOULD NEVER ASK AT THE END OF AN INTERVIEW:
  - "What does the company do?" (you should know this; research it)
  - "How much does the role pay?" (ask the recruiter, not the interviewer)
  - "How many vacation days do I get?" (ask HR, not the engineering team)
  - "When will I hear back?" (you can ask this, but not as your ONLY question)
  - Nothing (always ask something; saying "I have no questions" is a mistake)

RULE: Prepare 4-6 questions per round. You'll likely only get to 2-3.
      Rotate based on what was covered in the interview.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. QUESTIONS FOR ENGINEERING INTERVIEWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ABOUT THE TECHNICAL WORK:
  "What does a typical day look like for an engineer on this team — what
  percentage is feature work vs. infrastructure vs. on-call?"

  "What are the biggest technical challenges the team is facing right now?
  What's the hardest problem you've had to solve in the last 6 months?"

  "How does the team approach technical debt? Is there dedicated time for it
  or does it compete with feature work?"

  "What does your deployment pipeline look like — how often do you ship,
  and what's the path from commit to production?"

  "How does the team handle incidents and on-call? How often are engineers
  paged, and what's the philosophy around reducing alert fatigue?"

  "What does your observability stack look like? How do you monitor and
  debug production issues?"

  "How do engineers collaborate on design decisions? Do you do design docs,
  architecture reviews, or RFCs?"

ABOUT ENGINEERING CULTURE:
  "How does code review work on this team? What does a PR typically look like?"

  "How does the team balance engineering quality (testing, documentation,
  refactoring) with shipping speed?"

  "Are there any recent technical decisions the team is proud of? Or any
  you wish you had made differently?"

  "How much autonomy do individual engineers have in choosing solutions vs.
  following team-wide standards?"

  "What tech stack decisions are you wrestling with right now? Are there
  migrations or modernization efforts underway?"

ABOUT THE INTERVIEWER PERSONALLY:
  "What do you like most about working here? What's kept you here?"

  "What's something you'd change about the engineering culture if you could?"

  "What was the most interesting project you've worked on since joining?"

  "What surprised you most after joining — something you didn't expect from
  the outside looking in?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. QUESTIONS FOR YOUR POTENTIAL MANAGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ABOUT MANAGEMENT STYLE AND TEAM DYNAMICS:
  "How do you prefer to give and receive feedback? How often do you do 1:1s
  and what's the format?"

  "How do you typically handle it when an engineer disagrees with a technical
  or product decision you've made?"

  "How do you support engineers in their career development? Have any of your
  direct reports been promoted recently?"

  "What does success look like for this role in the first 30/60/90 days?"

  "What are the biggest challenges you see for the person coming into this role?"

  "What kind of person tends to thrive on your team? What kind of person
  tends to struggle?"

ABOUT TEAM COMPOSITION AND HEALTH:
  "How long has the current team been together? What's the tenure range?"

  "How does the team handle disagreements — do you have a formal process
  or does it work organically?"

  "What's the team's relationship with product management like? How are
  priorities decided?"

ABOUT GROWTH AND CAREER:
  "What would you need to see from me to consider me for a promotion in
  the next 12-18 months?"

  "Are there opportunities to grow into tech lead or architect responsibilities
  on this team?"

  "Do engineers here ever move between teams? Is there a formal rotation program?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. QUESTIONS FOR SENIOR ENGINEERS OR TECH LEADS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "What's the most technically interesting project the team is working on
  right now? What are the design challenges?"

  "If you were starting the system from scratch knowing what you know today,
  what would you do differently?"

  "What's the team's philosophy on microservices vs. monolith / service boundaries?"

  "How do you approach data modeling for [the core domain of this team]?"

  "What's your biggest engineering concern for scale over the next 2 years?"

  "How does the team stay current on new technologies? Are there dedicated
  time or channels for exploration?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. QUESTIONS FOR PRODUCT MANAGERS OR CROSS-FUNCTIONAL INTERVIEWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "How does the engineering team influence the product roadmap? How are
  priorities set and who has the final say?"

  "How does the team handle technical requirements that compete with
  product feature work (e.g., security, reliability, scalability)?"

  "What's the typical lifecycle of a feature from idea to shipped product?"

  "How does the team define and measure success for a feature post-launch?"

  "What does a strong engineering-product partnership look like on this team?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUESTIONS FOR HR / RECRUITING DURING THE OFFER PHASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "What's the typical timeline for my level to move to the next level?
  How are promotions decided?"

  "What's the performance review process — how are engineers evaluated
  and how often?"

  "What training and development resources are available? Is there a
  budget for conferences or courses?"

  "Could you walk me through the full benefits package? What are the health
  plan options?"

  "What does the typical week look like in terms of remote/in-office balance?
  Is that formally defined or left to the manager's discretion?"

  "For the equity component — what is the current stock price (or 409A
  valuation for private companies), and what is the refreshed grant policy?"

  "Is there a signing bonus clawback? What are the terms?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. QUESTIONS TO UNDERSTAND TEAM/COMPANY HEALTH (ADVANCED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These questions help you spot red flags. Ask tactfully.

  "What's your engineering team's attrition been like over the last year?"
  (High attrition = red flag; listen for honest vs. evasive answer)

  "Are there any significant reorgs planned, or has the team been stable?"
  (Frequent reorgs = instability or poor planning)

  "How many engineers are on this team? Is headcount growing or flat?"
  (Flat or shrinking headcount with the same scope = overloaded team)

  "What happened to the last person who was in this role?"
  (Great insight into why the role is open; "promoted" vs. vague answer)

  "How does senior leadership communicate company direction to the
  engineering team?" (Shows transparency culture)

  "What's the biggest risk to the company's success right now?"
  (Tests honesty and strategic awareness; evasive answer is informative)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. QUESTIONS TO CLOSE THE INTERVIEW ROUND WELL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Near the end of EVERY round, ask:

  "Based on what you've seen today, do you have any concerns about
  my candidacy that I can address?"

  WHY: This is high-risk, high-reward. If they have a concern, you get to
  address it on the spot instead of getting a silent rejection.
  Worst case: they say "no concerns" and you get a positive signal.
  Best case: they say "I was unsure about X" and you get to reframe it.

  FOLLOW-UP:
  "Is there anything from our conversation today that you'd like me to
  expand on or clarify?"
