================================================================================
INTERVIEW PREPARATION STUDY PLAN — 30 DAYS TO SENIOR JAVA DEVELOPER
================================================================================
Role Target: Senior Java Developer / Full-Stack Engineer
Telecom Domain: BSS/OSS, CDR processing, billing, subscriber management
Experience Level: 8+ years

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Each day = 2–3 hours of focused study
- Bold items = highest priority; interview most likely to ask these
- Review the cheat sheets at the END of each file before your interview
- Practice saying answers out loud; time yourself (target: 2 min per question)
- Behavioral questions: always use STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

================================================================================
PRIORITY CHEAT SHEET — TOP 30 MUST-KNOW QUESTIONS
================================================================================

JAVA CORE (most asked):
1. How does HashMap work internally? (hashing, buckets, treeify at 8)
2. What is the difference between == and equals()? Why override hashCode()?
3. Explain volatile, synchronized, and when to use each
4. How does CompletableFuture work? What is supplyAsync?
5. What are virtual threads (Java 21)? How do they differ from platform threads?
6. Explain streams: lazy evaluation, terminal vs intermediate operations
7. What is type erasure in generics?
8. How does the JVM garbage collector work? What is GC pause?
9. What is the happens-before relationship in the Java Memory Model?
10. Explain Optional: why use it? When not to use it?

SYSTEM DESIGN (most asked):
11. Design a rate limiter (token bucket vs leaky bucket vs sliding window)
12. How do you design a notification system at scale?
13. Explain CAP theorem with a practical example
14. What is eventual consistency? Give a real example
15. Kafka vs RabbitMQ: when to use each?
16. How do microservices communicate? Sync vs async trade-offs?
17. What is the Saga pattern? Choreography vs orchestration?
18. How would you design the CDR billing pipeline in your current company?
19. Explain CQRS. When is it worth the complexity?
20. How do you handle data consistency across microservices?

ENGINEERING PRACTICES (senior focus):
21. How do you handle technical debt? How do you prioritize it?
22. What does good code review look like from both sides?
23. How do you ensure production reliability? SLO and error budget approach?
24. Describe your approach to TDD
25. How do you mentor junior developers?

BEHAVIORAL (always asked):
26. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a technical decision (and how it resolved)
27. Describe a major technical challenge you faced and how you solved it
28. Tell me about a system you designed from scratch
29. How have you improved engineering culture or practices at your team?
30. Why are you looking for a new role? (have a positive, forward-looking answer)
