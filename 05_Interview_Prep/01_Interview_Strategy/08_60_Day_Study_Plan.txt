KEY NUMBERS TO MEMORIZE (Back-of-Envelope)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1 million requests/day   = ~12 req/sec
  1 billion requests/day   = ~12,000 req/sec
  1 day                    = 86,400 seconds ≈ 100K sec
  1 TB                     = 1,000 GB = 10^12 bytes
  L1 cache access          = 0.5 ns
  RAM access               = 100 ns
  SSD read                 = 0.1 ms
  HDD seek                 = 10 ms
  LAN round trip           = 0.5 ms
  WAN round trip (US)      = 150 ms
  1 Gbps network           = 125 MB/s
  Average web page         = 1-3 MB
  Photo (compressed)       = 300 KB
  Video (1080p, 1 hr)      = 1.5 GB
  Tweet                    = 300 bytes
  100M DAU × 1 action/day  = 1,000 req/sec
