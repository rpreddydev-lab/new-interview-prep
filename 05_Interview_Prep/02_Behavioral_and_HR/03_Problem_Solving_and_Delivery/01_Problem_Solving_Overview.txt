THE CORE THESIS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Great engineers aren't just smart — they deliver. The difference between a
"10x engineer" and an average one often comes down not to raw intelligence,
but to how they structure problems, navigate obstacles, maintain momentum
under pressure, and own outcomes from start to finish.

This section covers the behavioral competencies that assess your ability to
DELIVER in complex, ambiguous, high-stakes environments.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE 5 DELIVERY COMPETENCIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. PROBLEM DECOMPOSITION
   Can you break a large, vague problem into a tractable structure?
   → Identify root causes vs. symptoms
   → Prioritize sub-problems by impact and complexity
   → Define a path to a solution under uncertainty

2. OWNERSHIP AND INITIATIVE
   Do you treat your work as YOUR problem to solve — not someone else's?
   → You don't wait to be assigned every task
   → You escalate early when stuck but drive to resolution yourself
   → You care about outcomes, not just activities

3. RESILIENCE AND ADAPTABILITY
   Can you recover when things don't go as planned?
   → You stay solution-focused when the plan breaks down
   → You learn from failure and adjust
   → You don't let perfect be the enemy of done

4. PRIORITIZATION UNDER PRESSURE
   Can you make smart trade-offs when you have more work than time?
   → You distinguish urgent from important
   → You protect the highest-value work even when low-value tasks pile up
   → You communicate trade-offs rather than silently dropping work

5. QUALITY AND CRAFT
   Do you consistently deliver at a high quality bar while hitting deadlines?
   → You don't treat quality as optional under pressure
   → You build in testing, documentation, and observability
   → You can articulate what "good enough" means for a given context

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW THESE COMPETENCIES ARE ASSESSED BY SENIORITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
JUNIOR (IC1–IC2):
  • Can execute a clearly scoped task independently
  • Asks good questions before getting stuck for too long
  • Delivers what was asked; minor course corrections expected

MID-LEVEL (IC3):
  • Can scope a feature end-to-end
  • Identifies and resolves blockers independently
  • Delivers on commitments with reasonable predictability

SENIOR (IC4):
  • Delivers complex, multi-month projects across teams
  • Handles ambiguity and scope changes gracefully
  • Helps others unblock and delivers while doing so

STAFF/PRINCIPAL (IC5+):
  • Drives multi-team, multi-quarter initiatives
  • Sets the standard for delivery quality in the org
  • Identifies systemic delivery problems and fixes them

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS FOR DELIVERY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ✓ You define success metrics BEFORE starting
  ✓ You identify risks early (not at deadline)
  ✓ You communicate progress proactively (not only when asked)
  ✓ You make trade-offs explicit rather than hiding them
  ✓ You own the outcome, not just your tasks
  ✓ When you fail, you do a post-mortem and apply the lessons
  ✓ You find creative paths when the obvious path is blocked

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
COMMON DELIVERY QUESTION TYPES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. "Tell me about your most challenging project and how you delivered it."
2. "Describe a time you had to deal with ambiguous requirements."
3. "Tell me about a time you failed to deliver on time. What happened?"
4. "Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information."
5. "Tell me about a time you had to reprioritize mid-project."
6. "Describe a time you went above and beyond what was expected."
7. "Tell me about a time you had to balance speed and quality."
